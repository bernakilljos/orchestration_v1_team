Apply the **OODA Loop** framework (John Boyd) to analyze and decide:

**OBSERVE** — What are the raw facts? What data do we have? What's actually happening?

**ORIENT** — What does this mean? Apply mental models, past experience, context. What biases might we have? What are we missing?

**DECIDE** — Given the above, what are the options? Which is best and why?

**ACT** — Concrete next steps. Who does what, by when? What triggers a reassessment?

Be analytical. Surface assumptions. Prioritize speed of decision over perfection.
