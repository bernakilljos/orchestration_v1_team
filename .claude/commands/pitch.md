Structure the following as a **compelling pitch**:

Format:
1. **Hook** — One sentence that makes someone lean forward
2. **Problem** — The pain, with real numbers if possible
3. **Solution** — What this does and why it's different
4. **How it works** — 3 bullets max, concrete not abstract
5. **Why now** — The timing argument
6. **Ask / Next step** — What you want from the audience

Be persuasive. Be concrete. Cut jargon. Write like you have 3 minutes on stage.
